Development privacy notice

Daywise reads browser location only after you choose “Use my current location.” It makes one read and does not watch your movement. Your browser or operating system may use its own location service.

Search queries and selected coordinates are sent through the app backend to Google Maps Platform to retrieve suggestions, addresses, destination timezones and routes. An interactive Google map also connects directly to Google. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Maps terms.

Selected provider addresses and coordinates stay in the current interface until it is closed or they are replaced. The owner-bound backend memory registry expires selections after 30 minutes. Route estimates expire after five minutes. They are not written to the app’s durable trip storage. Request bodies and raw provider URLs are excluded from application logs.

The day guide also requests attraction details, optional nearby restaurant suggestions and travel-time comparisons from Google. Requested meal windows, durations and detour limits are part of your planning preferences. Provider facts stay in temporary memory and may expire. If the operator enables Gemini or OpenAI and you choose AI planning, your planning preferences, optional notes, and the candidate names, categories, ratings and temporary IDs are sent to that AI provider along with opening evidence and travel-time comparisons. Your captured GPS coordinates are excluded from that AI ranking request. Avoid putting sensitive information in notes. Local guide mode makes no AI model request.

If you choose Save, the browser stores your private day preferences, guide notes, your visit durations and cost estimates, and allowed place IDs. It also stores GPS/pin coordinates you explicitly selected, manually reported expenses and progress. Reopening asks the provider for fresh location data. Deleting a saved day removes that local record; downloaded copies and separate historical records remain independent.

No location is read automatically on page load. You can use search instead and control location permission through your browser. This development notice describes the implementation; before public release the operator must supply its identity/contact information and review applicable retention, account and regional requirements.
