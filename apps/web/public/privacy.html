Development privacy notice

Daywise reads browser location only after you choose “Use my current location.” It makes one read and does not watch your movement. Your browser or operating system may use its own location service.

Search queries and selected coordinates are sent through the app backend to Google Maps Platform to retrieve suggestions, addresses, destination timezones and routes. An interactive Google map also connects directly to Google. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Maps terms.

Only accepting an itinerary saves it. Accepted snapshots include selected addresses and coordinates, routes, prices, guide preferences and manual progress/expenses. Hosted snapshots are stored privately in Firestore through authenticated server endpoints; each record is restricted to its anonymous Firebase owner. Local development stores them in the local backend file instead. LocalStorage keeps itinerary IDs and pending-save IDs, not the full itinerary. IndexedDB keeps a device backup for recovery when the server is unavailable. Opening a saved itinerary fetches its private database record by ID where possible. Browser refresh never requests GPS automatically. Draft edits and generated previews are not saved as accepted trips.

Firebase anonymous authentication persists in browser IndexedDB to keep access across restarts on the same profile. It does not provide cross-device login, and clearing authentication data can lose access to that anonymous account. Legacy accepted device snapshots are backed up before migration to ID references and may be uploaded to the private database. A failed database save retains the device backup and offers retry. Deleting an itinerary removes its database content, device backup and ID reference; an ID-only server tombstone prevents delayed writes from recreating deleted content. Other accepted itineraries remain intact. Downloaded/shared copies are independent. Saved traffic and location evidence keep their original expiry labels and must be refreshed explicitly before new routing; saved snapshots are not current traffic. Request bodies and raw provider URLs are excluded from application logs.

The day guide also requests attraction details, optional nearby restaurant suggestions and travel-time comparisons from Google. The Include meal setting, midday timing rule, eating duration and detour limits are part of your planning preferences. Facts in the local trip snapshot keep their original checked times and may expire. Locations must be refreshed before editing a restored route. If the operator enables Gemini or OpenAI and you choose AI planning, your planning preferences, optional notes, and the candidate names, categories, ratings and temporary IDs are sent to that AI provider along with opening evidence and travel-time comparisons. Your captured GPS coordinates are excluded from that AI ranking request. Avoid putting sensitive information in notes. Local guide mode skips AI ranking. Separately, when the operator enables local festival checks, selecting a destination and travel date sends its city identity, state, country and month to Gemini with Google Search, even if AI itinerary ranking is unchecked. GPS/start coordinates, visit lists, notes and personal preferences are excluded from this lookup. Public event dates and source links are held in temporary caches for up to one hour; provider search suggestions display in an isolated frame. This check does not generate or save an itinerary.

For admission and meal allowance conversion, the server may request a dated reference exchange rate from Frankfurter. This request contains currency codes only; it does not contain your location, trip, identity or notes. Rounded prices and their original source dates are included in the local trip snapshot. If you enable price sharing, the reviewed copy includes per-visit admission and meal allowances.

Share trip can publish an explicit read-only snapshot with a random link in Firestore. Anyone with that link can view it until its seven-day expiry or creator deletion; it does not update with later private edits. Private endpoints are excluded unless explicitly included. Share trip also previews a static text copy before you copy, download or open device sharing. Start/return addresses and coordinates are excluded by default. Guide notes, child ages, dietary preferences, reported expenses and credentials are always excluded. WhatsApp sharing opens an external draft containing the reviewed schedule and Google Maps route links; you choose the recipient and send it. The links disclose selected visit coordinates and, only when opted in, starting/return coordinates. The shared map preview follows that same choice. Opening WhatsApp or Google Maps connects to that service; route links recompute directions rather than updating your saved schedule. No location is read automatically on page load. You can use search instead and control location permission through your browser. This development notice describes the implementation; before public release the operator must supply its identity/contact information and review applicable retention, account and regional requirements.
