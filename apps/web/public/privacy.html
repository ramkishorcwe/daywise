Development privacy notice

Daywise reads browser location only after you choose “Use my current location.” It makes one read and does not watch your movement. Your browser or operating system may use its own location service.

Search queries and selected coordinates are sent through the app backend to Google Maps Platform to retrieve suggestions, addresses, destination timezones and routes. An interactive Google map also connects directly to Google. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Maps terms.

The browser automatically saves the current trip on this device, including selected addresses and coordinates, the draft and accepted itinerary, price allowances, and reported progress and expenses. Refresh restores this local snapshot without another GPS read. Delete local trip removes the snapshot and its linked named preferences. Downloaded or shared copies remain independent. The owner-bound backend memory registry expires selections after 30 minutes. Route estimates expire after five minutes. These expiry times remain visible after browser restoration; a saved snapshot is not fresh traffic or a newly registered location. Provider responses are not written to the backend’s durable trip storage. Request bodies and raw provider URLs are excluded from application logs.

The day guide also requests attraction details, optional nearby restaurant suggestions and travel-time comparisons from Google. The Include meal setting, midday timing rule, eating duration and detour limits are part of your planning preferences. Facts in the local trip snapshot keep their original checked times and may expire. Locations must be refreshed before editing a restored route. If the operator enables Gemini or OpenAI and you choose AI planning, your planning preferences, optional notes, and the candidate names, categories, ratings and temporary IDs are sent to that AI provider along with opening evidence and travel-time comparisons. Your captured GPS coordinates are excluded from that AI ranking request. Avoid putting sensitive information in notes. Local guide mode skips AI ranking. Separately, when the operator enables local festival checks, selecting a destination and travel date sends its city identity, state, country and month to Gemini with Google Search, even if AI itinerary ranking is unchecked. GPS/start coordinates, visit lists, notes and personal preferences are excluded from this lookup. Public event dates and source links are held in temporary caches for up to one hour; provider search suggestions display in an isolated frame. This check does not generate or save an itinerary.

For admission and meal allowance conversion, the server may request a dated reference exchange rate from Frankfurter. This request contains currency codes only; it does not contain your location, trip, identity or notes. Rounded prices and their original source dates are included in the local trip snapshot. If you enable price sharing, the reviewed copy includes per-visit admission and meal allowances.

Separately, if you choose Save day preferences, the browser stores your private day preferences, guide notes, your visit durations and cost estimates, and allowed place IDs. It also stores GPS/pin coordinates you explicitly selected, manually reported expenses and progress. Reopening asks the provider for fresh location data. Deleting a saved day removes that local record; downloaded copies and separate historical records remain independent.

Share trip previews a static text copy before you copy, download or open device sharing. Start/return addresses and coordinates are excluded by default. Guide notes, child ages, dietary preferences, reported expenses and credentials are always excluded. WhatsApp sharing opens an external draft containing the reviewed schedule and Google Maps route links; you choose the recipient and send it. The links disclose selected visit coordinates and, only when opted in, starting/return coordinates. The shared map preview follows that same choice. Opening WhatsApp or Google Maps connects to that service; route links recompute directions rather than updating your saved schedule. No location is read automatically on page load. You can use search instead and control location permission through your browser. This development notice describes the implementation; before public release the operator must supply its identity/contact information and review applicable retention, account and regional requirements.
