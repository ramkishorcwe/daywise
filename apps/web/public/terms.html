Development use terms

Daywise helps select real locations and review provider driving routes. Driving estimates and your visit allowances can change. A route does not verify venue hours, ticket prices, dietary suitability, accessibility or total spending. Check relevant venue information and use an appropriate navigation service for directions.

Google Maps Platform content and external Google Maps navigation are subject to Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google’s Privacy Policy. Daywise does not replace those services’ terms. Read the Daywise privacy notice for the app’s handling of selected locations.

Accepting a route saves a private database snapshot with an ID reference and device backup; it does not book a venue or transport. Booked/actual amounts and progress are your manual, unverified reports. Saved preferences may require new provider lookups, and browser storage may be cleared or unavailable.

This is a development implementation, not authorization for a public traveler release. The operator must review these drafts, supply its identity/contact information, configure provider accounts and complete the outstanding release checks before publishing.
